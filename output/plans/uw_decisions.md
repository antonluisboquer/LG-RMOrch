# Step 2: Initial & Updated Underwriting Decisions

Access the full decision document from the Encompass eFolder under
'Underwriting Decision'. Review each new/updated decision and take required action.

## Important Rules
- An automated email is sent to the client when the UW issues the decision;
  replies land in the RM inbox. A copy is available to the client in the TPO Portal.
- TRID files need a credit decision (approved or denied) within **30 days** of the
  Initial Submittal Date (TPO Information screen).
- The loan must be back in UW with all Prior-to-Approval (PTA) conditions by the
  **25th day** after the Initial Submittal Date. If all PTAs are not received by
  day 25, submit for cancellation (see Step 1.3 DFT).

## Substeps

### 2.1 — Approvals
Call `review_approval`. Review the Conditional Approval:
- Ensure all conditions are understood; clarify unclear conditions with the UW and
  request the verbiage be updated when necessary.
- Act on anything needing attention (appraisal corrections, lock adjustments).
- Check for a **Misc-Counteroffer** condition — if present, ensure the Client/AE are
  aware and get the loan restructured as required.

### 2.2 — Suspense / Recommendations for Denials
Call `review_suspense`. The UW contacts the client/team before suspending. Reach out
on initial suspense and **weekly** thereafter to clear PTA conditions. The tool tracks
the 30-day decision window and the day-25 resubmit/DFT deadline from the Initial
Submittal Date.
